# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark*, organized into 29 dated sections. Each section includes the prose version, a corresponding literary quotation version, and local source references.

[Download the Word document](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx)

The quotation version contains 799 passages, including 677 by Russian authors. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The references now follow the numbered ACM-style system used in *What Takes Hold*: bracketed citation numbers, consistent source numbers, italic titles and hanging indents. All 799 citations link to the relevant source entry within their section; citation tooltips give the source locations. Each section retains full source details and passage locations, and its reference numbers link to a complete list of 30 numbered source entries at the end.

The historical dates, original prose and quotation wording are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments, citation numbering and local references. All citation targets and passage locations have been verified, and all 55 rendered pages have been reviewed.
