# Russian Ark

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark* and a corresponding literary quotation version, each organized into 29 sections.

Two Word editions are available:

- [Sourced version followed by references and prose](Russian_Ark_Sourced_Version_References_and_Prose.docx): the current edition contains the complete sourced literary version, followed by all references, then the complete prose version. Both versions use only Roman numerals I–XXIX for section headings, without descriptive titles or dates. The opening “Sourced literary version” label has been removed. The literary version forms new sentences and paragraphs by juxtaposing exact fragments, with capitalization and punctuation adapted at their boundaries. It follows the [editorial rules](EDITORIAL_RULES.md) adopted from *What Takes Hold*, including the author's exceptions for essential short excerpts.
- [Versions paired within each section](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx): an earlier edition in which each prose section is followed by its quotation version, with references collected at the end. This edition retains its earlier quotation choices and 677 distinct references. All 63 rendered pages were reviewed when it was produced.

The current literary version contains 848 cited fragments from 30 works, including 707 fragments by Russian authors. Sources follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. Quotations, their citation markers and their complete reference entries share a colour by the original work's publication or stated composition century: eighteenth century brown (#8A4B20), nineteenth century blue (#245A81), and twentieth century green (#2F6B45). The prose version remains black. Every narrative word in the sourced version comes from the cited texts. Source fragments can be trimmed and rearranged, with separate citations for their distinct spans. Editorial ellipsis markers are omitted.

The references retain the bracketed citation style, italic titles and hanging indents used in *What Takes Hold*. Numbers identify distinct source passages and run consecutively across all sections in order of first appearance. Different passages from the same book receive separate numbers; an exact repeat from the same source location reuses its number. In the current edition, 848 citation occurrences link to 708 distinct passage references, numbered [1]–[708], with 140 repeat occurrences. Every reference appears after the complete sourced literary version and includes source details and the passage location; citation tooltips also give the locations.

The chapter order and complete original prose are preserved. Historical and bibliographical dates remain in the body and references; dates have been removed from section headings at the author's request. The sourced paragraphs have been recomposed. All source wording and required centuries have been checked, and new source locations have been verified. Citation numbering and targets pass verification, with no numbering gaps or broken citation links. The latest heading and colour changes leave all narrative wording, reference wording and citation links unchanged. All 60 rendered pages of the current edition have been visually reviewed.
