# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark*, organized into 29 dated sections. Each section includes the prose version, a corresponding literary quotation version, and local source references.

[Download the Word document](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx)

The quotation version contains 799 numbered passages, favoring Russian literature. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The latest revision improves 17 further quotation pairings in the final five sections. The replacements give closer matches for the repeated confession, children heard nearby, a mother watching her son, the farewell and the palace seeming to move at sea. They also remove unrelated names, pronouns and incidental actions.

The historical dates and original prose are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments, numbering, and local references, and all 51 rendered pages have been reviewed.
