# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark*, organized into 29 dated sections. Each section includes the prose version, a corresponding literary quotation version, and local source references.

[Download the Word document](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx)

The quotation version contains 799 numbered passages, including 676 by Russian authors. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The latest revision improves nine quotation pairings in sections 15, 16 and 27. Seven passages from Tolstoy’s *After the Dance* (written 1903) bring closer parallels for the mazurka, remembered dance steps, shared pleasure and the transition toward military unease. Gorky and Proust also give closer matches for leaving together and treating an artwork as a confidant.

The historical dates and original prose are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments, numbering, and local references, and all 52 rendered pages have been reviewed.
