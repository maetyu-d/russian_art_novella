# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark* and a corresponding literary quotation version, each organized into 29 dated sections.

Two Word editions are available:

- [Sourced version followed by references and prose](Russian_Ark_Sourced_Version_References_and_Prose.docx): the complete sourced literary version comes first, all references follow, and the complete prose version comes last. Dates appear in parentheses in the chapter headings of both versions. The separate dating context and quotation century paragraphs have been removed. All 59 rendered pages have been reviewed.
- [Versions paired within each section](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx): each prose section is followed by its quotation version, with all references collected at the end. All 63 rendered pages have been reviewed.

The quotation version contains 799 passages, including 677 by Russian authors. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The references retain the bracketed citation style, italic titles and hanging indents used in *What Takes Hold*. Numbers identify distinct quoted passages and run consecutively across all sections in order of first appearance. Different passages from the same book receive separate numbers; an exact repeat from the same source location reuses its number. The 799 citation occurrences link to 677 distinct passage references, numbered [1]–[677], with 122 repeat occurrences. Every reference appears after the complete sourced literary version and includes complete source details and the passage location; citation tooltips also give the locations.

The historical dates, original prose and quotation wording are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments and citation numbering. All citation targets and passage locations have been verified, with no numbering gaps or broken citation links. The separated edition preserves every quotation paragraph, reference entry and prose paragraph from the paired edition.
