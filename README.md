# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark*, organized into 29 dated sections. Each section includes the prose version and a corresponding literary quotation version. All source references are collected after the final quotation section.

[Download the Word document](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx)

The quotation version contains 799 passages, including 677 by Russian authors. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The references follow the numbered ACM-style system used in *What Takes Hold*: bracketed citation numbers, consistent source numbers, italic titles and hanging indents. All 799 citations link to a complete list of 30 numbered source entries at the end. Each entry includes its source details and locations grouped by section and passage number; citation tooltips also give the source locations.

The historical dates, original prose and quotation wording are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments and citation numbering. All citation targets and passage locations have been verified, and all 47 rendered pages have been reviewed.
