# Russian Ark novella

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark*, organized into 29 dated sections. Each section includes the prose version, a corresponding literary quotation version, and local source references.

[Download the Word document](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx)

The quotation version contains 799 numbered passages, including 677 by Russian authors. Quotations follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. They provide thematic parallels to the prose rather than literal retellings of every detail.

The latest revision improves four quotation pairings in section 11. Tolstoy supplies closer parallels for a familiar person made strange and surprise at a spoken word. Two passages from Pushkin’s *The Captain’s Daughter* (1836), translated as *Marie*, preserve an adverse judgment of a poem and the wish to follow departing people.

The historical dates and original prose are preserved. All 799 passages have been checked for wording, century assignments, numbering, and local references, and all 52 rendered pages have been reviewed.
