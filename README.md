# Russian Ark: After Sokurov

An unseen visitor wanders through the Hermitage, where centuries coexist and history slips from room to room. Composed entirely of literary fragments from the eras it traverses, and drawn chiefly from the corpora of Russian and Soviet literature, Russian Ark: After Sokurov journeys through art, memory, imperial splendour and loss towards a sea without a shore.

Every narrative word in the sourced version comes from the cited texts. Source fragments may be trimmed and rearranged, with separate citations for their distinct spans. The current literary version contains 848 cited fragments, including 707 fragments by Russian and Soviet authors. Sources follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. Quotations, their citation markers and their complete reference entries share a colour by the original work's publication or stated composition century: eighteenth century brown (#8A4B20), nineteenth century blue (#245A81), and twentieth century green (#2F6B45).
