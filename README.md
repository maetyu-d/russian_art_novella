# Russian Ark

A prose adaptation of *Russian Ark* and a corresponding literary quotation version, each organized into 29 dated sections.

Two Word editions are available:

- [Sourced version followed by references and prose](Russian_Ark_Sourced_Version_References_and_Prose.docx): the current edition contains the complete sourced literary version, followed by all references, then the complete prose version. Dates appear in parentheses in the chapter headings of both versions. The literary version now forms new sentences and paragraphs by juxtaposing exact fragments, with capitalization and punctuation adapted at their boundaries. It follows the [editorial rules](EDITORIAL_RULES.md) adopted from *What Takes Hold*, including the author's exceptions for essential short excerpts.
- [Versions paired within each section](Russian_Ark_Story_with_Literary_Quotations.docx): an earlier edition in which each prose section is followed by its quotation version, with references collected at the end. This edition retains its earlier quotation choices and 677 distinct references. All 63 rendered pages were reviewed when it was produced.

The current literary version contains 848 cited fragments from 30 works, including 707 fragments by Russian authors. Sources follow each scene’s implied century; contemporary and undated scenes use twentieth-century literature. Every narrative word comes from the cited texts. Source fragments can be trimmed and rearranged, with separate citations for their distinct spans. Editorial ellipsis markers are omitted.

The references retain the bracketed citation style, italic titles and hanging indents used in *What Takes Hold*. Numbers identify distinct source passages and run consecutively across all sections in order of first appearance. Different passages from the same book receive separate numbers; an exact repeat from the same source location reuses its number. In the current edition, 848 citation occurrences link to 708 distinct passage references, numbered [1]–[708], with 140 repeat occurrences. Every reference appears after the complete sourced literary version and includes source details and the passage location; citation tooltips also give the locations.

The historical dates, chapter order and complete original prose are preserved. The sourced paragraphs have been recomposed. All source wording and required centuries have been checked, and new source locations have been verified. Citation numbering and targets pass verification, with no numbering gaps or broken citation links. All 60 rendered pages have been visually reviewed; the complete prose pages remain pixel-for-pixel unchanged.
